About the studio

From our hatchery,
to your shipped products.

Plagued By Poultry started with a handful of gamers chasing the strange, atmospheric worlds they couldn’t find anywhere else.

… That itch became a studio, with developers, artists, and storytellers. We split our time between original games and contract work for other teams that need the extra hands.

What we do

Whimsical, dedicated, always professional. Every project, ours or a client’s, gets the same obsessive attention to feel, from first meeting to market ready.

Who we are

A close-knit flock that plugs into bigger productions without missing a beat. Need concept art, a UI pass, or someone to finish the level nobody else has time for? We’re used to jumping in mid-flight.
